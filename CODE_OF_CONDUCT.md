# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want AppKit to be a welcoming, respectful, and useful place to collaborate. We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment:

- being respectful of differing opinions and experience levels
- giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully
- assuming good intent while still addressing harm clearly
- focusing criticism on ideas, code, and outcomes rather than people
- helping newcomers understand the project
- documenting decisions so future contributors are not left guessing

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- sexualized language or imagery
- personal attacks or insulting comments
- trolling or sustained disruption
- publishing private information without explicit permission
- dismissive behavior toward people asking reasonable questions
- any conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, commit messages, documentation, community chat, and any other place where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that do not align with this code of conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainer.

If the repository has a private security or contact channel enabled, use that channel. If not, contact the maintainer through the profile or organization associated with the repository.

Reports should include:

- what happened
- where it happened
- who was involved
- any relevant links, screenshots, or timestamps
- whether you need immediate action or privacy considerations

Maintainers will make reasonable efforts to review reports promptly and handle them with care.
